# Schema Templates

Use JSON-LD in populated artifacts.

## FAQPage (required for B and D)
- Include 5-7 question/answer pairs.
- Questions should map to user intent around service, cost, timeline, and fit.

## HowTo (conditional)
- Include when workflow/process explanation exists.
- Use practical steps that mirror delivery process.

## Local Business / Organization signals
- Include brand entity, service nouns, and target locations where applicable.
- Keep facts verifiable; avoid exaggerated claims.
