# Vertical Compliance Rules

Apply to all outputs.

## Medical (HWG-sensitive)
- Avoid guaranteed outcomes.
- Avoid prohibited comparative or misleading efficacy claims.
- Use factual, educational phrasing.

## Legal (RVG-sensitive)
- Avoid promise language about case outcomes.
- Use informational language on scope and process.
- Keep pricing references aligned with applicable legal framing.

## Consulting
- Use precise, professional tone.
- Prioritize measurable business outcomes over hype.

## NEO THE AGENCY
- Keep service language aligned to approved NEO offerings.
- Preserve conversion-oriented but non-spammy tone.

General:
- No unverifiable superlatives.
- No absolute guarantees.
- Prefer transparent constraints and expectations.
